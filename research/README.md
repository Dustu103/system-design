## 🔍 Research Principles

1. **Primary Sources First**: Prioritize SEC filings, official engineering blogs, and postmortems over third-party summaries.
2. **Empirical Grounding**: Back architectural arguments with academic studies (e.g., MIT/Harvard) and empirical codebase data (e.g., CAST Software, Sourcegraph).
3. **No Fluff**: Keep quotes exact, dates verified, and dollar figures citable.
